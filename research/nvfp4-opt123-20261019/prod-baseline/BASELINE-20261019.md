## Dual-GPU vs single-GPU (2026-10-19 21:35-21:47, 512K, vision resident on both sides)

Same binary, same script, cold read per point, 400 generated tokens, thinking=medium.

| ctx | prompt | prefill single | prefill dual | decode single | decode dual | decode gain | step single | step dual |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 40K | 40,163 | 7,630 | 5,794 | 119.0 | 140.0 | +17.6% | 23.6 | 18.4 |
| 128K | 127,557 | 7,822 | 6,841 | 118.0 | 156.0 | +32.2% | 22.6 | 16.8 |
| 256K | 255,007 | 7,383 | 6,871 | 119.0 | 155.9 | +31.1% | 22.8 | 17.7 |
| 384K | 382,676 | 6,923 | 6,539 | 109.4 | 144.8 | +32.4% | 21.1 | 15.7 |
| 512K | 511,858 | 6,478 | 6,020 | 113.7 | 155.7 | +36.9% | 23.9 | 17.5 |

- Decode: dual is 18-37% faster; prefill: dual is 5-24% slower (worst at 40K, narrows to 7% at 512K). Keep dual.
- The 40K dual prefill 5,794 is the first request in that run (cold page cache); later points settle at 6.5-6.9K.
- Single-GPU fallback is kept: `strata-nvfp4-single.json` + `run-nvfp4-single.sh` (vision resident on GPU0, `--expert-cache 5350`).
- `start_prod.sh` picks the config by visible GPU count, so a pulled card needs no manual edit.
- The Monitor page only lists `gpu_list`, so the helper GPU shows no utilization there even while it works (log shows `CUDA1: N expert entries`, power 24->50 W).
